## [RepetitionCurse Exposes Fragile MoE Routing](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=MzI3MTA0MTk1MA==&amp;mid=2652713777&amp;idx=3&amp;sn=01c4801592c61a1f52ae653764b5ac39) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A Hong Kong University of Science and Technology team reported RepetitionCurse at ICML 2026, showing that highly repetitive token inputs can concentrate many tokens on a small number of experts. This disrupts load balancing and can create GPU stragglers in Mixture-of-Experts models. MoE models improve scalability by activating only selected experts, so routing imbalance can reduce their practical efficiency and increase inference or training delays. The finding highlights a potential systems vulnerability for large-language-model deployments that receive repetitive or adversarial inputs. The reported mechanism is that repeated tokens produce highly similar hidden representations, making the router more likely to assign consecutive tokens to the same experts. The provided reports describe the load-imbalance effect, but do not include quantitative results, affected model versions, or mitigation details.

rss · 新智元 · Jul 18, 10:26

**Background**: Mixture-of-Experts models contain multiple expert networks and use a router to select a subset of them for each token. This conditional computation increases model capacity without executing every expert for every input. Load balancing is important because routing most tokens to a few experts can leave other experts idle while creating bottlenecks. Common approaches include noisy Top-k routing and an auxiliary load-balancing objective.

Agent evaluations commonly inspect both final answers and intermediate tool calls, making these invariants important testing targets.
