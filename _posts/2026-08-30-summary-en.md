## [Tencent Open-Sources Hy4 Preview, a 770B-Parameter MoE Model](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-releases-and-open-sources-tencent-hy4-preview/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Tencent released and open-sourced Hy4 preview, a next-generation large language model with 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion active parameters, and a context window exceeding one million tokens. Tencent also reported that the model participated in optimizing training methods, data strategies, evaluation frameworks, and low-level operators, creating an early recursive self-improvement loop. Hy4 suggests that advanced models may increasingly assist with the engineering work used to train and evaluate successor systems, potentially accelerating model development. Its reported rapid adoption on OpenRouter also indicates strong early demand for high-capability open models with relatively low inference and caching costs. Hy4 is an early preview rather than an independently validated final release: available benchmark results are largely self-reported, and the model is known to produce overly long reasoning and excessive self-verification. Community discussion also highlighted its reported trillions of processed tokens within days and a 5% cache cost, while raising concerns that aggressive token-density optimization could reduce linguistic ambiguity and expressive depth.

**Background**: A mixture-of-experts model contains many parameterized subnetworks but activates only a subset for each input, allowing a large total model to operate with lower per-request computation. Model training involves selecting data, optimizing the learning process, evaluating results, and implementing efficient low-level operations. Recursive self-improvement in this context means using the model within bounded, experiment-driven development cycles; it does not by itself demonstrate unrestricted autonomous intelligence growth.

## [NASA’s Roman Telescope Prepares to Survey the Wide-Field Infrared Universe](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/roman-space-telescope/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

NASA’s Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is scheduled to launch on August 30, 2026, aboard a Falcon Heavy rocket. The mission will use wide-field observations to investigate dark energy and discover exoplanets. Roman could greatly expand the amount of sky surveyed compared with narrow-field observatories, accelerating studies of cosmic expansion, dark energy, galaxy evolution, and planetary systems. Its observations are expected to benefit both professional researchers and the broader public because processed data will be openly available. Its Wide Field Instrument is a 300-megapixel infrared camera with a field of view about 200 times larger than Hubble’s WFC3/IR camera, while offering comparable sensitivity and spatial resolution. The mission is based on an existing 2.4-meter primary mirror donated by the National Reconnaissance Office, and comments cite plans for fully open data with no embargo.

**Background**: Dark energy is the name given to the poorly understood component associated with the universe’s accelerated expansion. Roman will observe large populations of galaxies and supernovae across wide areas of the sky, providing measurements that can test how cosmic expansion changes over time. Exoplanets are planets outside the Solar System, and wide-field infrared imaging helps researchers find and characterize faint or distant objects.

The Department of Homeland Security reportedly used a legal mechanism known as a 1509 summons to obtain or seek sensitive records from journalists, nonprofits, and unions. The reported targets included phone and communications records, raising questions about whether the authority was used beyond its narrow customs-investigation purpose. The allegations create a significant civil-liberties and press-freedom concern because government access to confidential communications can expose sources, chill reporting, and intimidate advocacy groups. The dispute also tests how much oversight is required when agencies seek private records directly from telecommunications and technology companies. ## [Samsung Advances Processing-in-Memory for AI Workloads](https://chipsandcheese.com/p/hot-chips-2026-samsungs-processing) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Samsung’s Hot Chips 2026 presentation describes processing-in-memory technology that places computation inside DRAM to reduce data movement for AI workloads. The design operates as a highly constrained SIMD processor, with the operation, scale factor, and one source operand shared across memory banks. Data movement often consumes substantial energy and limits performance in AI accelerators, so computing closer to stored data could improve efficiency. However, the narrow programming model and application-specific behavior may limit adoption beyond workloads that fit its execution pattern. Samsung allows PIM registers to be written in single-bank mode, but this capability is intended for debugging rather than normal operation. The main tradeoff is reduced flexibility: applications must organize dependent data and operations to match the memory-bank structure, which can create significant implementation and software-development costs.

hackernews · ingve · Aug 29, 06:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49487341)

**Background**: Processing-in-memory, or PIM, integrates limited processing capability into memory devices so that some calculations can occur without moving all data to a separate processor. This approach targets the data-movement bottleneck associated with conventional computer architectures, especially in AI workloads that repeatedly process large matrices and model parameters. Unlike a general-purpose processor, PIM typically supports a restricted set of operations and data-access patterns.

## [Record Ocean Heat Coincides With Developing Strong El Niño](https://www.latimes.com/environment/story/2026-08-26/highest-ever-ocean-temperature-measured-as-powerful-el-nino-forms) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Global sea-surface temperatures reached their highest recorded level as a powerful El Niño developed. Copernicus reported a global average of about 70°F \(21°C\) on August 22, 2026, exceeding the previous single-day record from March 2024. Exceptional ocean warmth can intensify climate risks and, together with El Niño, influence weather patterns, ocean conditions, and marine fisheries worldwide. The development may increase pressure on communities facing heat, energy demand, water costs, and disruptions to marine ecosystems. The record refers to global sea-surface temperature, a measurement that can vary with the observing method and sampled depth; instruments include satellites, buoys, ships, and other platforms. El Niño is a naturally occurring ocean-atmosphere pattern, and the specific influence of human-caused warming on its strength remains an active research question.

hackernews · measurablefunc · Aug 29, 23:26 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49494231)

**Background**: El Niño is the warm phase of the El Niño–Southern Oscillation, or ENSO, which involves changes in winds and sea-surface temperatures across the tropical Pacific. Warming in the central and eastern tropical Pacific can alter weather patterns, ocean conditions, and fisheries far beyond the region. Sea-surface temperature is measured near the ocean surface using instruments such as satellite radiometers, buoys, ships, and ocean gliders.
