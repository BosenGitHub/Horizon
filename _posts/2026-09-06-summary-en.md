## [Declarative Attention Lets Language Models Control Their Own Context](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1w7sgf3/language_models_can_control_their_own_attention_r/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The paper introduces Declarative Attention, a protocol that lets a language model declare whether it needs global, focused, or local attention during generation. On 15 long-context tasks, zero-shot tests reduced attended tokens by 52.0% on Gemma-4-31B and 31.1% on Qwen-3.6-27B, with accuracy drops of 1.27 and 2.75 percentage points, respectively. The approach targets a major cost of million-token inference: repeatedly scanning the full KV cache even when only a small context region is relevant. Because the declarations are generated by the model and interpreted by the inference engine, the method offers a new intrinsic route to sparse attention without requiring a separate token-scoring mechanism. Declarative Attention uses three modes: global reads the full context, focus reads a specified region, and local reads only recent output. Unlike proxy-based selection, which still has O\(N\) cost per step, the protocol can skip most KV-cache reads, but the reported accuracy trade-offs and zero-shot evaluation indicate that reliability and scaling remain important limitations.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/eigenlaplace · Sep 5, 06:07

**Background**: In transformer language models, the KV cache stores key and value representations for previously processed tokens so they can be reused during decoding. Attention determines which cached tokens influence the next generated token; scanning a very large cache can therefore become expensive. Declarative Attention asks the model to identify the context scope it needs, allowing the inference engine to avoid reading irrelevant cached tokens.

## [Qwen3.8 Flash Next Templates Compared on SWE-bench](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1w84mod/qwen38_flash_next_templates_comparison/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A controlled test compared stock, Fixed, and Sharp chat templates for Qwen3.8 Flash Next on the same 100-task SWE-bench Verified slice. With mini-SWE-agent 2.4.6, the stock template resolved 99 tasks at xhigh reasoning, Fixed resolved 98, and Sharp resolved 94. The results show that chat-template choice can materially affect coding-agent accuracy and token usage, even when the model and benchmark remain unchanged. They also suggest that the popular alternative templates do not universally outperform the stock template, making reproducible evaluation more useful than anecdotal impressions. The runs used a Ryzen 9 9900X, 128 GB of RAM, an RTX PRO 6000 WS, CUDA 13.3, full 262K context, BF16 KV cache, a 51.2 GB FP8 n-gram table, and 32 GB of HiCache. The comparison is limited to one 100-task slice, and the reported results show that higher reasoning effort substantially increased output and reasoning tokens.

reddit · r/LocalLLaMA · HeDo88TH · Sep 5, 16:02

**Background**: SWE-bench Verified is a benchmark for evaluating AI agents that modify software repositories to solve real-world issues. A chat template defines how conversations and tool-related instructions are formatted before being sent to a language model, so changing it can alter the model’s behavior without changing the model weights. SGLang’s HiCache extends KV-cache reuse into host memory, which helps support long-context inference under the test configuration.
