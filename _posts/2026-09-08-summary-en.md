## [China’s Supreme Court Clarifies Liability for AI-Related Disputes](https://www.cnr.cn/news/20260907/t20260907_527806795.shtml) ⭐️ 9.0/10

On September 7, China’s Supreme People’s Court released a 24-article judicial interpretation on artificial intelligence disputes. It addresses AI-generated faces and voices, algorithmic price discrimination, impersonated endorsements, autonomous driving, privacy violations, and intellectual property issues. The interpretation gives developers, platforms, advertisers, and content businesses clearer legal boundaries for deploying generative AI and automated systems. It may also strengthen protections for personal identity, privacy, consumer fairness, and intellectual property as AI-generated content becomes more widespread. Using AI to create identifiable facial or voice features without consent may constitute an infringement of personality rights, while algorithmic price discrimination may trigger liability when it harms consumer rights. The interpretation also allows legally supported punitive-damages claims in cases where AI impersonation of another person’s endorsement induces consumption, and addresses privacy abuses such as doxxing and online human searches.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 7, 09:32

**Background**: AI face-swapping and voice cloning are forms of deepfakes that use artificial intelligence to imitate a person’s recognizable appearance or voice. Algorithmic personalized pricing uses consumer data and automated models to offer different prices or treatment to different users; when this becomes unfair price discrimination, it is often described in China as “killing the familiar.” These technologies can create disputes because people may be impersonated, profiled, or treated differently without meaningful consent or transparency.

## [TPUv7 Ironwood Challenges NVIDIA on Inference Economics](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/tpu-inferencex-full-steam) ⭐️ 8.0/10

SemiAnalysis published the first third-party InferenceX Official Preview results for Google’s TPUv7 Ironwood, comparing it with NVIDIA’s B200 and B300. The analysis claims Ironwood delivers up to 50% better performance per dollar across much of the performance Pareto curve. If independently validated, the results could lower inference costs and intensify competition between Google’s TPU platform and NVIDIA’s dominant GPU ecosystem. Ironwood is especially significant because Google is offering it for external workloads through outright purchases and Google Cloud rentals. The initial comparison uses Qwen3.5 397B in FP8 and relies on the developing native TorchTPU vLLM serving stack, with future support planned for models such as Kimi K3 and GLM5.3. The external software stack is still maturing, particularly for speculative decoding, disaggregated prefill, KV-cache offloading, and multi-turn agentic workloads.

**Background**: Ironwood, also called TPUv7, is Google’s inference-optimized TPU generation. A TPU is a specialized accelerator designed for machine-learning workloads, while inference is the process of running a trained model to generate outputs. The comparison focuses on performance per dollar, which relates serving throughput or latency to the hardware or cloud cost required to deliver it.

## [China Plans 6G Commercialization and Expanded Advanced Communications](https://36kr.com/newsflashes/3973030022541575) ⭐️ 8.0/10

China’s Ministry of Industry and Information Technology issued the Information and Communications Industry Development Plan for the 15th Five-Year Plan period. It calls for continued 5G evolution, city-level dual-10-gigabit network development, timely initiation of 6G commercialization, and orderly applications of eSIM, out-of-network communications, satellite internet, and terminal agents. The plan sets a broad policy direction for China’s next communications cycle, potentially influencing network operators, device makers, satellite internet providers, and AI terminal developers. It also links faster networks with emerging device intelligence and alternative communication capabilities. The plan targets continuous 5G-Advanced coverage in urban areas above the county level and extension into key townships, with peak speeds of up to 10 Gbps downlink and 1 Gbps uplink in urban hotspots. It also proposes domestic connectivity rules for satellite internet devices, real-world trials for new mobile intelligent terminals, and regulatory capabilities for terminal agents; the announcement does not provide a specific 6G launch date.

Out-of-network communication refers to device-to-device communication that does not depend on a conventional base station or wireless router. An eSIM is an embedded SIM that performs the subscriber-identification function without requiring a removable physical card.

## [Huawei Unveils Kirin 9050 Pro After Six Years](https://www.news.cn/20260907/adf46c5c003240d28cc3cf6de54f9b5f/c.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Huawei unveiled the Mate XT 2 tri-fold smartphone in Guangzhou on September 7, featuring the new Kirin 9050 Pro processor. The chip is described as the first high-performance processor to use logic-folding technology, which layers logic units and adds vertical interconnects to shorten signal paths and reduce latency. The launch marks Huawei’s return to releasing a new flagship Kirin processor at a major product event after six years. Its logic-folding approach could provide a new path for improving chip performance and interconnect efficiency, with implications for domestic high-performance chip design and advanced packaging. Huawei says the Kirin 9050 Pro reorganizes logic units within a single chip into layered structures rather than simply adding complete chips, with vertical channels shortening internal signal routes. However, the available report provides no process node, benchmark scores, power figures, or detailed implementation data, so the claimed performance gains cannot yet be independently assessed.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 7, 08:20

**Background**: Traditional chip layouts mainly distribute logic units across a two-dimensional plane, so signals may need to travel through relatively long interconnects. Logic folding places parts of the logic structure in layers and uses vertical interconnects to make communication paths more direct. Similar three-dimensional integration concepts are associated with shorter global interconnects, higher bandwidth, and lower power, but the report does not establish that this chip uses conventional 3D stacking.

## [OpenAI Researchers’ AI Usage and Token Costs Surge](http://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260907-ai-use-inside-openai/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI disclosed that, as of August 2026, median daily token usage costs among its researchers exceeded $600, while the top 10% exceeded $7,000 per day. Researchers’ output-token volume was 124 times higher than on November 1, 2025, and about 70% were running at least four AI agents simultaneously. The figures suggest that frontier AI research is shifting toward intensive, multi-agent workflows with substantially higher computational costs. They may influence how organizations measure researcher productivity, allocate compute budgets, and design internal AI tools. The report is based on a secondary account of OpenAI’s disclosure, and the figures describe token usage costs rather than necessarily direct cash spending by individual researchers. Multiple AI agents can collaborate through tools, knowledge bases, memory, or code interpreters, but the disclosure does not specify the models, tasks, or accounting method used.

An AI agent is a system built around a language model that can pursue a task and use tools, while multiple agents can coordinate on a larger workflow. Agent-based systems therefore can increase throughput while also multiplying model calls and token consumption.
