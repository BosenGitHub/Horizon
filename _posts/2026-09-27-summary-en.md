## [Intel Panther Lake Ships 18A Manufacturing Milestone](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/intel-panther-lake-teardown) ⭐️ 8.0/10

SemiAnalysis tore down Intel’s Panther Lake, examining its 18A process, PowerVia backside power delivery, RibbonFET gate-all-around transistors, and Foveros-S packaging. The product moves Intel’s manufacturing recovery from roadmap claims to shipped silicon. Panther Lake provides concrete evidence of Intel’s progress in rebuilding competitive semiconductor manufacturing, while testing whether its advanced process and packaging technologies can compete with leading foundries. Its results could affect Intel’s product competitiveness, foundry credibility, and the broader adoption of backside power delivery. The teardown found similar logic density between Panther Lake’s 18A compute logic and TSMC N3E GPU logic, but 18A does not lead TSMC N3P, N2, or Samsung SF2 in peak density. Panther Lake combines 18A compute tiles, GPU tiles built on Intel 3 or TSMC N3E, TSMC N6 I/O tiles, and a passive base tile through Foveros-S; PowerVia improves power routing but adds capacitance, thermal resistance, and process complexity.

rss · SemiAnalysis · Sep 26, 13:36

**Background**: Backside power delivery moves the main power network behind the transistor layer, separating power routing from frontside signal wiring and potentially reducing resistance. Intel calls its implementation PowerVia, while RibbonFET is Intel’s name for a gate-all-around transistor structure in which the gate surrounds the channel, improving electrostatic control as features shrink. Foveros is Intel’s three-dimensional chiplet packaging technology for stacking specialized tiles into a single package.
